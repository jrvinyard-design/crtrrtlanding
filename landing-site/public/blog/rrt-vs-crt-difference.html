RRT vs CRT: What's the Actual Difference?

If you're new to respiratory therapy licensure, the CRT/RRT naming can be genuinely confusing at first. Here's the clear version, plus how it maps to the actual exams you'll take.

The short version

CRT and RRT aren't two separate, competing credentials — they're sequential tiers of the same credentialing pathway through the NBRC (National Board for Respiratory Care).

CRT — CERTIFIED RESPIRATORY THERAPIST

The entry-level credential. Earned by passing the TMC Examination at the "low cut score." This is required to become licensed and practice as an RT in most states.

RRT — REGISTERED RESPIRATORY THERAPIST

The advanced credential. Earned by passing the TMC at the higher "high cut score," which then makes you eligible to sit for the Clinical Simulation Exam (CSE). Passing both gives you the RRT.

How the exams connect

  • Everyone starts with the TMC Examination — a 140-item, scenario-based multiple-choice exam
  • Scoring at the low cut score earns the CRT credential
  • Scoring at the high cut score earns CRT and makes you eligible for the CSE
  • Passing the CSE (a branching clinical simulation exam) on top of a high-cut-score TMC earns the RRT

In other words: you can't skip to RRT directly. The path always runs through the TMC first, and how well you score there determines whether the CSE is even on the table.

Does it actually matter for your career?

Practically speaking — yes, in a few concrete ways:

  • Pay: many employers offer a pay differential for RRT over CRT-only therapists
  • Job eligibility: some positions, particularly in critical care, NICU, or leadership-track roles, list RRT as a requirement or strong preference
  • State licensure: requirements vary by state, but many states specifically reference the CRT as the minimum credential for licensure, with RRT as an additional distinction

Worth knowing: this TMC/CSE structure is changing. Starting in 2027, the NBRC is combining both exams into a single new RT Examination. If you're planning your exam timeline now, it's worth checking which track applies to you based on when you're testing.

Bottom line

CRT and RRT aren't competing paths — RRT is simply the deeper credential you unlock by performing well on the same starting exam. If your goal is RRT, the strategy is straightforward: study to the high cut score standard from day one, since you won't know which tier you've hit until you get your TMC results.
